description: 'Rex: https://www.superlogical.com/


  Everyone''s comparing Rex to herdr, Orca, and tmux, and writing it off for what
  it''s missing. I think that''s the wrong comparison. Rex is a terminal multiplexer
  first, and it''s building the primitive everything else sits on.


  I almost didn''t try it. I had all my workflows built around herdr, and I wanted
  stability, not another tool to switch to. But after digging in, I think Rex is the
  one to bet on. In this video I explain what a multiplexer actually is, why every
  agent tool needs one, how Rex''s mirroring differs from tmux, and why I''m building
  my workflows around it.
